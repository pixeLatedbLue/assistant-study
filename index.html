How should an assistant respond?

This study asks how people judge requests made to a conversational assistant. You will read short sequences of user messages and decide whether the final request should be answered. Your answers help us study agreement and ambiguity. They are a separate perception study, not the official safety labels.

It takes about 5 minutes for 10 questions. There are no right or wrong answers.

Content warning Some messages ask for harmful or sensitive things. No assistant answer text is shown. Participation is voluntary. You can skip an item or stop at any time; answers already submitted are retained for this study.

Your name is required so the study organiser can see who participated. It is stored privately with your visit number and can be linked to your answers. Names are excluded from the answer-analysis export. No email or account is required.
